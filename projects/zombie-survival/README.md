# Project 001 — Zombie Survival

**Status:** In development  
**Engine:** Godot 4 (2D)  
**Language:** GDScript  
**AI tools:** ChatGPT + Codex

## Concept

A dark, cinematic 2D zombie survival game set on rain-soaked streets in an abandoned city. Survive increasingly dangerous hordes for ten minutes, upgrade your weapons and abilities, and defeat the final boss, **The Behemoth**.

## Planned gameplay

- WASD movement, aim-aware camera, automatic shooting, and Space dodge.
- Pistol, shotgun, and assault rifle; manually activated grenade (Q) and Molotov (E).
- Walker, Runner, Tank, and Spitter enemies.
- XP pickups, level-up choices, supply crates, and weapon evolutions.
- Dynamic horde pacing and a ten-minute final boss.
- Atmospheric city streets, rain, flickering lights, and interactive obstacles.

All features above are **planned**, not completed.
